Experiences

Discover moments of relaxation, adventure, and fun with our swimming pool, garden retreats, gaming arcade, and beachside escapes.

Swimming Pool

Dive into our crystal-clear swimming pool with heated zones and a separate kids’ splash area. Relax under private cabanas with complimentary tropical drinks, enjoy our poolside snack bar offering fresh fruit and finger foods, or join our morning aqua aerobics sessions for a fun start to your day. Sun loungers, fluffy towels, and underwater LED lights add a luxurious touch to your swim experience.

Garden View

Wander through our lush gardens featuring blooming hibiscus, orchids, and bonsai trees. Enjoy guided nature walks, afternoon yoga classes on the lawn, or quiet reading corners by koi ponds with trickling waterfalls. Seasonal flower workshops and outdoor picnic spots make the garden perfect for both relaxation and family fun.

Gaming Arcade

Enter our vibrant arcade packed with classic pinball machines, racing simulators, VR games, and multiplayer consoles. Compete in daily tournaments, try your luck at our prize-winning claw machines, or enjoy retro games like Pac-Man and air hockey tables. Snacks and drinks are available, plus arcade credits are complimentary for guests staying over 2 nights.

Beachside Escape

Step onto our golden sandy beach with turquoise waters and sunbeds lined with colorful umbrellas. Join guided paddleboarding, kayaking, or beach volleyball sessions, enjoy sunset yoga classes, or sip on tropical cocktails at our beachfront bar. Complimentary beach towels, waterproof speakers, and hammocks make your seaside relaxation unforgettable.
